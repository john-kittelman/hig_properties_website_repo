House

1540 Downing Street

Historic Denver character meets thoughtful modern updates in this beautifully remodeled two-story Victorian home. Rich architectural details including custom crown molding, stained and leaded glass windows, solid wood doors and trim, Victorian-style hardware, and ceiling rosettes bring warmth and personality throughout, while updated LVT flooring offers easy everyday living.

The main level features a welcoming living room with an electric insert set within the original fireplace, a spacious dining room, and an additional TV room or home office. The large kitchen offers generous wood cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and plenty of space for cooking and gathering. A charming Victorian-inspired guest bath completes the main level. Upstairs, the generously sized primary suite and additional bedrooms are conveniently located near the second-floor laundry. Additional features include an evaporative cooler, central vacuum system, ceiling fans, and abundant character throughout.

Outdoor spaces are equally inviting. Relax on the classic front porch overlooking a charming cottage garden, or enjoy the large covered back deck with a gas fireplace — an ideal extension of the living space for relaxing or entertaining. A small fully fenced yard and two off-street parking spaces add everyday convenience. This distinctive home offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the charm and craftsmanship of a historic Denver residence with the updates and functionality of modern living.
