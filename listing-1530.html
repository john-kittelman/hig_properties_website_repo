Downing Place Apartments

1530 Downing Street, Denver, CO 80218

Apartment Building • 19 Units

1530 Downing Street at Colfax Avenue

Downing Place Apartments in the historic Denver Capitol Hill. Downing Street and Colfax Avenue. Entrance to the front of the building is controlled by an electronic security system. Our small family owned and operated three level smoke free property contains 19 units with studio, one bedroom and two bedroom apartments.

Utilities

2025 minimum monthly bill was $61, maximum $86 (trash, water/sewer, gas). Xcel metered electricity (refrigerator, lights, personal electronics, etc.) is paid by the tenant.

Amenities

  • Spacious exercise room
  • Laundry facility for tenants
  • Secured sports equipment storage for large items such as a bike
  • Electronic security system at the front entrance
  • Smoke-free three-level building

Parking

Off-street parking is available alongside the building. Six months free off-street parking per vehicle.
